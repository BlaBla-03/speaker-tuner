    Checks you can do by ear
    
        
      
          
    • Left / right: sound should come only from that side. If not, the wiring or balance is swapped.
      • 
          
    • Polarity: "in phase" should sound centred and full. "Reversed" should sound hollow and wide. If they sound the other way round, one speaker is wired backwards.
      • 
          
    • Sweep: listen for buzzing or rattling panels, and for notes that suddenly jump out or vanish.

      🚗 Tuning your car
      
        
        
          
      1. Connect the iPhone by cable / wired CarPlay if you can (Bluetooth works but may switch to call quality while the mic is on).
        2. 
          
      2. Set the car's EQ, bass/treble, loudness and "sound effects" to flat / off. Balance and fader centred. Spotify EQ off.
        3. 
          
      3. Engine off (or idling), windows and doors closed, A/C fan low.
        4. 
          
      4. Profile: Car. Measure tab → "This phone". Start with a low volume and raise it until the meter is in the green zone.
        5. 
          
      5. Hold the phone at ear height in the driver's seat, mic (bottom of phone) pointing forward. Take 3–5 measurements, moving the phone ~10 cm each time.
        6. 
          
      6. EQ tab: copy the values into the car's EQ or Spotify's EQ (not both).
        7. 
          
      7. Measure again with the EQ on to check the result. Then adjust Bass/Treble preference to taste.
        8. 
        
      
      
      
      
      
        
      💻 Tuning your laptop
      
        
        
          
      1. Open this same website on the laptop → Signal tab.
        2. 
          
      2. On the iPhone: Profile Laptop, Measure tab → "Another device".
        3. 
          
      3. Laptop: press "Pink noise both" at a normal volume.
        4. 
          
      4. iPhone: hold it where your head is when using the laptop (~50 cm in front of the screen). Take 3 measurements.
        5. 
          
      5. EQ tab → Equalizer APO → Share/Copy the text and paste it into the config file on the laptop.
        6. 
        
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Good to know
      
        
        
          
      • Turn off silent mode on the iPhone, otherwise the phone may not play the test signal.
        • 
          
      • The iPhone mic is accurate from about 50 Hz to 14 kHz. Outside that range the app is careful.
        • 
          
      • Grey parts of a curve were too close to background noise to trust, so they're ignored.
        • 
          
      • EQ fixes tone, not distortion. If a speaker rattles, lower the bass, don't boost it.
